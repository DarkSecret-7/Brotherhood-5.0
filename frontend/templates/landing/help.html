How You Can Contribute

🤝

The Brotherhood is what we build together.

🛠
Technical
Help construct the Skill Graph infrastructure, develop assessment and matching tools, create federation management platforms, and build transparency and auditing systems.
🤝
Social
Form early federations in your community/industry, recruit workers who share the vision, document governance processes, and facilitate conflict resolution.
📚
Educational
Define skill structures in your domain, create learning resources, validate assessment rubrics, and connect theory to practice.
💰
Financial
Fund infrastructure development under structural constraints, support early federations with patient capital, help establish collective assets, and model non-extractive investment.
📢
Communication
Explain the vision to different audiences, document successes and failures, create educational content, and build connections with aligned movements.
🧠
Visionary
We are reimagining the social contract of work. If you write about labor economics, coordination failure, or decentralized governance, we want to hear your ideas.

The most important contribution: Use the infrastructure. Learn through the Skill Graph. Work through federations. Prove your capability through assessment.
